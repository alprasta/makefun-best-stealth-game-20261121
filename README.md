MOTHLIGHT is a top-down stealth game: you are Wisp, a moth-burglar slipping through three moonlit gardens, gathering every moonpetal and reaching the moon gate unseen. Lantern wardens patrol with visible cones of light, shadow shrubs hide you, and lamp pools expose you — hold SHIFT to sneak, break line of sight to shake a chase, and finish without raising a single alarm to earn the GHOST rating.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
Then open http://localhost:8080.
